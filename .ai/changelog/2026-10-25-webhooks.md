# Outgoing webhooks

- Date: 2026-10-25
- Area: api, worker, web, shared, docs
- Spec: §10

## Summary

Admins with `webhook.manage` configure endpoints that receive HMAC-signed POSTs for issue,
request, approval and comment events, scoped to the organization, a department or a team.
Deliveries go through a PostgreSQL outbox, are retried with exponential backoff and logged.

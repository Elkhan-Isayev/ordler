# Cycles (sprints)

- Date: 2026-10-24
- Area: api, web, shared
- Spec: §5.2 Cycles, §14 Phase 2

## Summary

Teams can plan fixed-length cycles: length, start weekday, cooldown and how many to plan
ahead. When a cycle ends, its unfinished issues roll over to the next one automatically and
history records it. Each cycle shows progress, a burn-up chart built from issue history, and
how many issues were added after it started.

## Changes

- `apps/api/drizzle/0016_cycles.sql` — `cycles`, `issues.cycle_id`, cycle settings on `teams`
  (additive; cycles are off until a team lead turns them on).
- `apps/api/src/cycles/` — planning helpers (UTC calendar dates), `CycleService` (idempotent
  planning and rollover under a per-team advisory lock, burn-up from history), routes, and a
  scheduler on worker replicas; page loads also plan lazily.
- Issues take `cycleId` (open cycles of the same team only), recorded in history including at
  creation; MCP `create_issue` accepts `cycle` ("current", "next" or a number).
- Web: cycle list and cycle page with burn-up (validated colours, legend, end labels,
  crosshair tooltip, table view), cycle picker on the issue page, in the new-issue dialog and
  the right-click menu, board/list filter by cycle, team settings → Cycles; i18n en/ru/de/az.
